Public Square Analytics and GovFeeds

Public Square Analytics is a technology and consulting firm dedicated to improving the digital public square. Our product, GovFeeds, turns research on government social media into a tool that communication teams use every day.

What GovFeeds does

GovFeeds lets city and county communication teams search what peer governments posted this week and see which posts residents responded to. Teams use it to borrow approaches that work, benchmark their own accounts, and bring evidence to managers and councils.

Visit GovFeeds Public Square Analytics

From research to practice

GovFeeds grew out of our NSF-funded research on digitally accountable public representation, which collects and analyzes the social media posts of public officials and local governments. The same data support academic studies of how governments communicate, how residents engage, and how AI-generated content is entering official communication.
