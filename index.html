About

I am the DeGrandis-McCourtney Early Career Professor in Political Science at Penn State, Director of the Center for Social Data Analytics, and an affiliate of the Institute for Computational and Data Sciences. My lab develops and applies social data science methods to study systems of policymaking and digital communication with policymakers.

I am also co-founder and CEO of Public Square Analytics, whose product GovFeeds helps local governments learn from each other on social media. I hold a Ph.D. from UNC Chapel Hill and taught at UMass Amherst before joining Penn State.

Research areas

Policy diffusion and networks

How policies spread across states, cities and countries, and the statistical methods (network event history analysis, latent network inference) that recover who learns from whom.

Digital communication and public officials

How elected officials and local governments communicate online, from misinformation and harmful content to how residents engage with official posts.

Network analysis and inferential methods

Statistical models for networks, including ERGMs, TERGMs and network-effects regression, with open-source software for applied researchers.

AI and machine learning for social science

Using machine learning and generative AI as research instruments, and measuring how AI is changing government and public communication.
