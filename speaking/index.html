Talks & Workshops

I give talks and workshops for universities, government associations, and industry teams. Recent hosts include University College Dublin, Ohio State, Vanderbilt, Michigan, Princeton, and Meta.

AI and the digital public square

How local governments and elected officials communicate online, what residents respond to, and how generative AI is changing public communication.

How policies spread

Evidence on policy diffusion across states and cities, and network methods for learning who learns from whom.

Network methods for social science

Network-effects regression, ERGMs, and causal inference with interdependent data. Available as talks or hands-on workshops.

AI agents in research

How AI agents are changing the workflow of empirical research, and how labs can use them while keeping results reproducible.
