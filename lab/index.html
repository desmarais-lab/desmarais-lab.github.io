The Lab

The lab develops computational and statistical methods (machine learning, network analysis, natural language processing, AI, and causal inference) and applies them to policymaking and digital communication with public officials. Our work has been funded by the National Science Foundation, the Russell Sage Foundation, and the Hewlett Foundation.

Prospective students

You are welcome to email me with a short description of your interests.
